Repayment Structure

Each placement is structured as a bilateral Income Share Agreement (ISA) between the investor and the funded pilot. Once the pilot enters paid employment, the investor receives 15% of gross annual base salary for a period of twenty years from the date of first disbursement. The obligation is discharged in full — regardless of the time elapsed — if and when cumulative repayments reach three times the principal advanced.

Repayment obligations are governed by a legally binding Income Share Agreement executed prior to disbursement. The agreement includes standard provisions covering assignment of future earnings, event-of-default triggers, and step-in rights, ensuring the investor's position is protected throughout the repayment period.
