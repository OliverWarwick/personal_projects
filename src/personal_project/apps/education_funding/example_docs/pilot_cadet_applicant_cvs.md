# Example Trainee Pilot CVs — Pre-Training Cadet Applicants

Five fictional but realistic example CVs representing the type of candidate who applies to UK airline-sponsored cadet programmes (BA Speedbird, Jet2FlightPath, easyJet MPL, etc.) before entering flight training. Each represents a distinct applicant archetype. No candidate holds a licence or significant flight hours — the CVs reflect the application stage, not post-training.

## Notes on Format and Context

These example CVs are illustrative documents for the education funding application tool. Key characteristics shared across all five:

- **No commercial pilot licence or hours** — all candidates are ab-initio or hold at most a PPL(A) or GPL
- **GCSEs met** — all candidates satisfy the minimum GCSE requirements (Maths, English, Science at grade 4+/C+) that sponsored programmes require
- **Evidence of aviation interest** — all show concrete action, not just stated interest (Air Cadets, trial lessons, gliding, ATPL theory started)
- **Character evidence** — work experience is chosen to demonstrate attributes airlines test for: composure under pressure, teamwork, communication, leadership
- **No degree requirement** — while Candidates 2, 4 have degrees, Candidates 1, 3, 5 do not; sponsored programmes do not require higher education
- **Age range** — covers the 18–28 window; sponsored programmes are open to applicants up to 58 but cadets are disproportionately in their late teens to late twenties
