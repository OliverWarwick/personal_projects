How it works

We put up the training capital you need to enrol on a sponsored cadet programme — ground school, flight hours, type rating, and the living costs that go with them. In return, you commit a fixed percentage of your post-qualification salary for an agreed period. The repayment share and term are capped before you sign, and the clock only starts once you're flying the line.

Who it's for

  • Applicants with a confirmed conditional offer on a UK sponsored cadet programme.
  • Holders of a valid Class 1 medical (or eligible to obtain one before training start).
  • UK residents committed to a full-time training pathway.

Repayment structure

Each placement is structured as an Income Share Agreement (ISA). Once the funded pilot enters paid employment, the programme receives 7% of gross annual base salary for a period of ten years from the date of first disbursement. The obligation is discharged in full — regardless of the time elapsed — if and when cumulative repayments reach three times the principal advanced.

Repayment obligations are governed by a legally binding Income Share Agreement executed prior to disbursement. The agreement includes standard provisions covering assignment of future earnings, event-of-default triggers, and step-in rights.
