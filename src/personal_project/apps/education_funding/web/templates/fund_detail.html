Repayment Structure

Each placement is structured as an Income Share Agreement (ISA). Once the funded pilot enters paid employment, the fund receives 15% of gross annual base salary for a period of twenty years from the date of first disbursement. The obligation is discharged in full — regardless of the time elapsed — if and when cumulative repayments reach three times the principal advanced.

Repayment obligations are governed by a legally binding Income Share Agreement executed prior to disbursement. The agreement includes standard provisions covering assignment of future earnings, event-of-default triggers, and step-in rights, ensuring the fund's position is protected throughout the repayment period.
